# Purpose of a Process First Enterprise Platform

Give the people who own business processes a way to define, run, and improve them in one place. An HR lead should be able to describe how a request works, publish a form and routing rules, and review the resulting cases without commissioning a custom app for every process.

## The product idea

The platform gives employees one place to submit requests and gives reviewers one place to act on them. Teams define their own processes using shared controls for ownership, approvals, and case history. Systems such as Salesforce or NetSuite can supply data or record an official transaction when a process calls for it.

## The first workflow

Use flexible-work requests as the first example. HR defines what employees submit, when a manager can decide, and when the case should go to HR. Employees submit the proposed schedule, dates, and work location. A request involving work from another country goes to HR after manager review. Reviewers can inspect the original information, ask for clarification, and record a decision.

## Ownership and decision authority

- The **function owner** assigns process ownership and controls who can publish within that function.

- The **process owner** maintains the definition, reviews sample cases, and proposes changes. They can publish if they have that authority.

- The **assigned reviewer** can see the information they need for their case and record the decision they are authorized to make.

Record who published each version, which version governed each request, and who made each case decision. AI can help draft forms and rules from a conversation, surface ambiguous steps before publication, and summarize evidence for a reviewer. Published rules govern routing, and the assigned reviewer makes the decision.

## The smallest useful product

Start with a process register, a simple editor with a sample-case preview, a form rendered from the published definition, and a work queue with case details. The editor captures the purpose, trigger, required information, decision roles, and exception path.

The first test is whether an HR lead can publish one process, run a routine case and an exception through it, review the collected information, and make a change for future cases. Use the pilot to establish which process elements recur before expanding the builder or connecting other systems.
